# ⚡ BOOSTLY

### 🎮 Understand your PC. Estimate your FPS. Play smarter.

The open-source tool to analyze your hardware, estimate gaming performance, and better understand your PC.

- 🌐 **Platform:** Web
- 🚧 **Status:** In development
- 💻 **Technologies:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- 🆓 **Price:** Free

*Free • Open source • No installation required • Accessible from your browser*

---

## 🌌 About Boostly

Boostly is an open-source project that aims to make PC performance analysis accessible to everyone.

Its goal is to help users better understand their hardware, test their browser's graphics performance, and get FPS estimates for their favorite games.

Whether you use a gaming PC, a laptop, or an Android device, Boostly aims to provide a simple, modern interface adapted to your device.

> 🚧 **This project is under development.** The features described below are planned goals and may not all be available yet.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🖥️ Hardware Analysis

Get information about your device that your browser is able to provide.

- 🔍 Processor information, when available.
- 🎨 Graphics information through WebGL, when available.
- 🧠 Memory-related information.
- 📱 Device type and certain browser capabilities.
- 🖥️ Screen information and resolution.
- ❓ Option to manually enter hardware components that cannot be detected.

> ℹ️ Browsers restrict access to certain hardware information. Exact detection of the CPU, GPU, and RAM is therefore not guaranteed.
